HANH VI PHO BIEN

Cac hanh vi vi pham pho bien
trong moi truong so

Viet, dang, chia se noi dung  sai su that, khong phu hop.
Lam lo thong tin ca nhan, to chuc gay anh huong xau.
Gui  thu rac, tin nhan rac, spam quay roi.
Su dung du lieu cua nguoi khac  khong xin phep.
Su dung phan mem  khong co ban quyen.

CANH BAO NGUY HIEM

Bat nat, lua dao
va thieu van hoa so

Bat nat qua mang (Cyberbullying): de doa, boi nho, co lap nan nhan tren khong gian mang.
Lua dao qua mang (Scam): gia danh, du do chuyen tien, danh cap tai khoan.
Ung xu thieu van hoa: chui boi, xuc pham, kich dong bao luc trong binh luan.

TAM QUAN TRONG

Vi sao phai su dung Internet dung luat?

Lop 7: Da biet chi ra vi du truy cap khong hop le vao cac nguon thong tin.
Lop 8: Da biet cach dam bao san pham so do minh tao ra the hien dao duc, van hoa va khong vi pham phap luat.
Nguoi su dung Internet, mang xa hoi  phai trang bi kien thuc can thiet de su dung khong gian mang co trach nhiem.

CAN CU PHAP LY

Hanh lang phap ly Viet Nam (2006 – 2013)

2006
Luat Cong nghe thong tin so 67/2006/QH11 do Quoc hoi ban hanh — quy dinh chi tiet ve quyen va trach nhiem cua to chuc, ca nhan tham gia ung dung va phat trien CNTT, cung nhu cac hanh vi bi nghiem cam.
2013
Nghi dinh so 72/2013/NĐ-CP do Chinh phu ban hanh — quy dinh chi tiet viec quan li, cung cap, su dung dich vu Internet, thong tin tren mang, tro choi dien tu tren mang; dam bao an toan thong tin va an ninh thong tin.

LUAT AN NINH MANG 2018

Luat An ninh mang &
cac hanh vi can tranh

Nam 2018, Quoc hoi ban hanh Luat An ninh mang — quy dinh ve hoat dong bao ve an ninh quoc gia va dam bao trat tu, an toan xa hoi tren khong gian mang.
Khong xem xet ki ma da chia se, phat tan thong tin chua kiem chung — hanh vi vi pham phap luat ve an ninh quoc gia, trat tu xa hoi.
Binh luan sai trai, chi tiet bia dat — gay hoang mang du luan, anh huong nghiem trong den xa hoi.

TRACH NHIEM CONG DAN SO

Quyen nhan than, tai san
& trach nhiem cua cong dan

Vi pham quyen nhan than, uy tin: dang hinh anh, dia chi nha, bien so xe… cua nguoi khac khi chua co su dong y.
Vi pham so huu tri tue, tai san: sao chep, phat tan noi dung co ban quyen khong xin phep; danh bac truc tuyen…
Trach nhiem: chu dong trang bi kien thuc,  kiem chung thong tin truoc khi chia se, ung xu van minh tren khong gian mang.
